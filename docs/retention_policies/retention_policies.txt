POLICY 1: MONTH-TO-MONTH CONTRACT

Customers on month-to-month contracts may be offered:
- A 10% discount for moving to a one-year contract.
- A 15% discount for moving to a two-year contract.
- Contract migration should not remove existing services unless the customer requests it.


POLICY 2: TECHNICAL SUPPORT

Customers without technical support may be offered:
- Three months of complimentary technical support.
- Priority support onboarding.
- A follow-up service review after 30 days.


POLICY 3: HIGH MONTHLY CHARGES

Customers with high monthly charges may be offered:
- A plan review.
- Removal of unused add-on services.
- A lower-cost bundled plan where eligible.
- Discounts must remain within approved pricing rules.


POLICY 4: NEW CUSTOMERS

Customers with tenure below 12 months may receive:
- Onboarding assistance.
- Service education.
- A first-year loyalty incentive.
- Proactive support contact.


POLICY 5: PAYMENT METHOD

Customers using manual payment methods may be encouraged to move to automatic payment.
Eligible customers may receive:
- A small billing discount.
- Payment reminders.
- Auto-pay setup assistance.


POLICY 6: RETENTION RESTRICTIONS

Retention agents must:
- Never promise unauthorized discounts.
- Never change a customer's plan without consent.
- Never use demographic traits such as gender or age to decide offers.
- Base recommendations on service usage, account status, billing, contract type, and support needs.
